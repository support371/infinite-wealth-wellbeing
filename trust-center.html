This page reports the platform's current operational controls. It does not present unpublished charters, policies, credentials, certifications, or service guarantees as though they have already been verified.

Implemented controls

  • Public inquiry and membership endpoints validate input and fail closed when staff workflows are not configured.
  • Workflow forwarding requires a signing secret.
  • Browser submissions are restricted to the configured public origin.
  • Submission endpoints use no-store responses and defensive browser headers.
  • Public forms use a honeypot field and explicitly warn against submitting highly sensitive data.
  • Payment-card collection remains disabled.

Not yet represented as complete

  • Privacy Policy and formal data-retention schedule.
  • Terms of Use and Membership Agreement.
  • Refund/Cancellation Policy.
  • Medical/well-being and financial-information disclaimers.
  • Credential, testimonial, impact-metric, ministry/tax, fiduciary, and service-claim verification.
  • Authenticated database, administrative review portal, and durable audit records.
Release rule: a missing control stays visible as missing. The production branch does not convert an intended policy or credential into a completed claim simply because the website has a place to display it.
