Release candidate

The platform is being completed under evidence gates.

The production branch is intentionally exposing only workflows whose behavior can currently be verified. Legacy service, health, financial, ministry/tax, credential, testimonial, membership-volume, and impact claims remain in internal review until supporting evidence and applicable policy review are complete.

Safe default: if a public service route has not passed its release gate, it resolves here instead of presenting an unverified claim as a finished operational service.

Available in this release candidate

  • Validated contact submission workflow.
  • Membership-interest application with no payment collection or automatic provisioning.
  • Evidence-based Trust Center status.
  • Donation status gate with payment collection disabled.

Still behind release gates

  • Paid membership and subscription provisioning.
  • Donation/payment checkout and receipt workflows.
  • Investment/advisory service claims and recommendations.
  • Health/well-being efficacy and practitioner claims.
  • Ministry/tax/PHA legal-status claims.
  • Testimonials, impact metrics, and credentials awaiting substantiation.
