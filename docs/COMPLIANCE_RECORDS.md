# Compliance Records

The Trust Center should support official records without claiming legal status until verified.

Recommended fields:

- title
- recordType
- issuingBody
- documentReference
- effectiveDate
- reviewDate
- status
- fileUrl
- reviewer
- approvalNotes
- publicVisibility
- auditTrail

Final tax-exempt status and regulatory claims must be verified against official records and professional/legal review.
